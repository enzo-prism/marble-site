About marble

marble is a free workout journal for iPhone and iPad that turns workouts you type or paste into logged sets, on the device. It is made by independent developer Lorenzo Quaid Sison, listed on the App Store as marble.fit, and its source code is public.

What it’s for

Most people already write workouts down somewhere: a note, a whiteboard photo, a coach’s message. marble starts there. You type or paste the session in your own words, marble shows you the sets it found, and nothing is saved until you have checked it. From then on it is a fast logbook with personal records, estimated 1RM, weekly goals and sprint times.

Principles

  • Your words first. Gym shorthand, sentences, a whole week of Notes, or a Hevy or Strong export all work.
  • Review before save. Every exercise match, set, date and note is shown to you before it becomes history.
  • Private by default. No account and no marble server. Apple Health and backups only happen when you choose them.
  • Whole and free. No paywall, no ads and no premium tier.
  • Show the math. The formulas behind the numbers are published, like the one-rep max estimate and the DOTS score.

How it’s built

marble is a native SwiftUI app that stores your training with SwiftData on the device. Gym notation and Hevy or Strong CSV files are read by an on-device parser on every supported iPhone and iPad. Where Apple Intelligence is available, its on-device model helps structure free-form sentences, and its readings are checked against what you wrote. Apple Health, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and the rest-timer Live Activity use Apple’s own frameworks.

Questions

Who makes marble?

marble is made by Lorenzo Quaid Sison, an independent iOS developer.

Why is marble called marble.fit on the App Store?

Searching the App Store for “marble” returns marble games, so the listing is named marble.fit. The app itself is called marble, and its website is marble-fit.app.

How do I get support or request a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub at github.com/enzo-prism/marble/issues. The app’s source code is public in the same repository.

Is marble really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no ads and nothing to unlock.
