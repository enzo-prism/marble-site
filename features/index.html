Features

Everything marble does.

Three tabs, a few iOS hooks, and nothing to sign up for. Free, with no account and no ads.

Add

Write it your way. Review, then save.

Typed, pasted, imported or scanned. Nothing is logged until you’ve checked it.

  • Paste or type in your own words

    Gym notation like Squat 5x5 @ 225, or a note as it is, with a live preview.

  • On-device parsing

    A deterministic parser on every device, and Apple Intelligence for prose where available.

  • Multi-day Notes

    Paste a week. Dates, weekdays and Day 1 labels split it into workouts.

  • Hevy and Strong CSV

    RPE, notes, failure sets and session times come across. Warm-ups are skipped.

  • Review before saving

    Check every match and set. A weak match becomes a new exercise, never a wrong merge.

  • Drafts that wait for you

    An unfinished review survives relaunches, with your edits intact.

  • Scan handwritten pages

    Photograph a training notebook. Text recognition runs on device.

  • History and repeat

    Search past sessions, and repeat one as an editable draft.

  • Shortcuts handoff

    Send text from Shortcuts straight into Add.

Log

Log without losing your flow.

Weight, reps, distance, time, RPE and rest, with only the fields each exercise needs.

  • Fast set logging

    Last time and your best, before you type anything.

  • Log Again

    Repeat your last set with one tap.

  • Save + Next

    Save a set and stay in the sheet for the next one.

  • Personal-record cues

    The record to beat, and a New PR cue the moment you pass it.

  • Workout sessions

    Start, log and finish a focused session.

  • Weekly split

    Give each training day a plan.

  • Sprints to the tenth

    Distance, reps and a target time, timed to 0.1 seconds.

  • Rest timer

    A Live Activity with +30 s and End, and a rest-complete alert.

  • Supplements

    Creatine, protein and daily habits beside your training.

  • Calendar

    Training days, streaks and progress photos, month by month.

  • Exercise library

    Search, favourite and edit exercises, each with its own icon.

Progress

Clear progress, computed on your phone.

Top lifts up front. Goals, charts and reports one tap deeper.

  • Top exercises

    Your five most-logged lifts, with record weights and dates.

  • Estimated 1RM verdicts

    Progressing, holding or adapted, compared at matched effort.

  • PR feed and rep records

    Every real record, and your best weight at each rep count.

  • Progression hints

    A suggested next load, with the evidence behind it.

  • Weekly goal and flex weeks

    Hit your target four weeks running to bank a flex week.

  • Monthly report

    Sessions, volume and records, against the same point last month.

  • Muscle coverage

    Weekly hard sets per muscle group, against the 10–20 set guide.

  • Bodyweight and DOTS

    Track weigh-ins and compare each lift to your bodyweight.

  • Daily Highlights

    A private evening recap, on the days you train.

iOS

Reach marble from anywhere on iOS.

A set is never more than a glance away.

  • Widgets

    Your weekly goal on the Home and Lock Screen, with quick logging.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Log a set, repeat it, or start and finish a workout by voice.

  • Control Center and the Action button

    Quick logging from Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.

  • Live Activities

    Rest counts down in the Dynamic Island, with +30 s and End.

  • Spotlight

    Find your exercises from system search.

  • iPhone and iPad

    iOS and iPadOS 26 or later, in light and dark, with larger text.

Apple Health

Connected to Apple Health, only if you choose.

Apple Watch and Garmin workouts come in through Health. marble has no Watch app of its own.

  • Import workouts

    Apple Watch and Garmin sessions, with duration, calories and heart rate.

  • Import bodyweight

    Weigh-ins from Health, for bodyweight trends and DOTS.

  • Send sessions to Health

    Strength workouts with an effort score, so they count toward Training Load.

  • An honest limit

    Health keeps workout summaries, not sets and reps. Those live in marble.

Privacy and data

Your training stays yours.

No account, no marble server, no ads and no third-party tracking.

  • On device

    Parsing, analysis and storage all run on the device, offline.

  • JSON backup and restore

    A portable backup. A restore adds what’s missing and never deletes.

  • Photos stay local

    Progress photos and videos never leave the device.

  • Careful recovery

    If saved workouts can’t open, the original files are kept, with retry and recovery.

  • Checkable claims

    The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and the source code is public.

  • Free

    Every feature, with no subscription and nothing to unlock.
