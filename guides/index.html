Guides

Guides to training with marble.

Practical, specific walkthroughs: moving your history in, writing workouts the way you already do, and connecting the devices you train with.

  • Import Hevy and Strong workouts Bring a Hevy or Strong CSV export into marble, check every match, and keep your history.
  • Log workouts from Apple Notes Paste a note, even a whole week of them, and turn gym shorthand into sets you review before saving.
  • A private, offline workout tracker What stays on your iPhone, what leaves only when you choose, and how to keep a backup without an account.
  • Keep a sprint training log Set a distance and a target time, time every rep to the tenth, and see your fastest times per distance.
  • Log strength training with Apple Watch Record the workout on Apple Watch or Garmin, log the sets in marble, and connect the two through Apple Health.
