Workout notation explained

In gym notation, sets come before reps: 3x10 means 3 sets of 10 reps. A load follows @, so Squat 5x5 @ 225 is five sets of five at 225. RPE 8 rates effort out of 10, about two reps short of failure, and AMRAP means as many reps as possible.

By Lorenzo Quaid Sison · Published November 24, 2026

The x is read as “by”. Sets × reps is the usual order, but some coaches write weight × reps, as in 135x5. When one number is clearly a load, it is the weight.

Weight, percentages and units

A number after @ is the load: Bench 3x8 @ 185. A unit makes it unambiguous, as in @ 100 kg or @ 185 lb. A percentage like @ 85% means 85% of your one-rep max, which you can estimate with the one-rep max calculator.

Effort: RPE and RIR

RPE (rating of perceived exertion) scores how hard a set was from 1 to 10. RIR (reps in reserve) counts the reps you had left, so RPE is roughly 10 minus RIR.

Rest, time and distance

  • Rest is written rest 90s or rest 2 min, between sets.
  • Timed sets use seconds or minutes: Plank 3x45s, 3x1:00.
  • Distance and time go together for cardio: Run 5k 25:00, Row 2000m 7:30.
  • Repeated efforts put the count first: Sprints 4x20m is four 20 m sprints.

AMRAP, EMOM, rounds and supersets

  • AMRAP: as many reps as possible. Pushups 3xAMRAP.
  • EMOM: every minute on the minute. EMOM 10 min: 5 burpees is ten rounds of five, one at the start of each minute.
  • Rounds: a circuit repeated. 3 rounds: 10 pushups + 15 squats is three passes through both.
  • Supersets: A1 and A2 alternate set for set, then B1 starts the next pair.

Tempo

Tempo gives the seconds for each phase of a rep. In tempo 3-0-1, you lower for three seconds, don’t pause, and lift in one. A fourth number, as in 3-1-1-0, adds a pause at the top, and X means as fast as possible.

How marble reads it

Paste notation like this into marble and it becomes sets you review before saving, on the device, without Apple Intelligence. A few specifics:

  • A weight without a unit uses your kg or lb preference. An explicit unit wins.
  • RPE 8, rpe9 and @RPE 8 set effort. A bare @ 8 is read as a weight.
  • rest 90 is 90 seconds, while rest 3 is three minutes.
  • A range like 4x8-10 logs the lower end and keeps the range in the set note.
  • A1 only sets the order. EMOM 10 min: 5 burpees logs 10 sets of 5.
  • Percentages such as @ 85% and tempo notes are ignored rather than logged as weights.

Questions

What does 3x10 mean in a workout?

3x10 means 3 sets of 10 reps. Sets come first and reps second, so you do 10 reps, rest, and repeat that twice more.

What does 5x5 mean?

5x5 means 5 sets of 5 reps, usually with the same weight on every set. It is a common strength format for squats, presses and rows.

What does @ mean in a workout?

The @ sign introduces the load: Squat 5x5 @ 225 means five sets of five at 225. When it is followed by RPE, as in @RPE 8, it gives the effort instead.

What does RPE 8 mean?

RPE is the rating of perceived exertion on a 1 to 10 scale. RPE 8 means you could have done about two more reps; RPE 10 means you could not have done another.

What does AMRAP mean?

AMRAP means as many reps as possible. 3xAMRAP is three sets where you do as many good reps as you can each time.

What does EMOM mean?

EMOM means every minute on the minute. EMOM 10 min: 5 burpees means starting 5 burpees at the top of each minute for 10 minutes, resting for whatever is left of the minute.

What do A1 and A2 mean in a program?

Letters group exercises and numbers give their order. A1 and A2 are a superset: do one set of A1, then one of A2, and repeat before moving on to B1.
