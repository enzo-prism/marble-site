Guide

A private, offline workout tracker for iPhone, with no account

marble keeps your training on your iPhone or iPad. There is no sign-up, no marble account or server, no ads and no third-party tracking. Logging, parsing and analysis all work offline, and you keep your own copy with a JSON backup.

What “private” means here

  • No account. You open the app and start logging. There is no email, password or profile.
  • No marble server. Your sets, sessions, supplements, weigh-ins and plans are stored on the device.
  • No ads or third-party tracking in the app.
  • Analysis runs on the device. Estimated 1RM trends, records, the monthly report and Daily Highlights are all calculated from your own sets on the iPhone.
  • Parsing runs on the device too. Typed and pasted workouts, CSV files and scanned pages are read locally. When Apple Intelligence structures a sentence, it does that on device as well.

How to check it

You don’t have to take the claims above on trust. marble’s privacy label on the App Store reads Data Not Collected, which is the developer’s declaration to Apple that the app collects nothing. And the app’s source code is public, so anyone can read what it does with your data.

Set it up

  1. Install marble from the App Store. It’s free and has nothing to unlock.
  2. Open it. There’s no sign-up step, only a short onboarding.
  3. Log. Add a workout in your own words, or log set by set in Log. None of this needs a connection.
  4. Decide about Apple Health. It’s off until you connect it. You can import workouts and bodyweight, or send completed sessions to Health, or do neither.
  5. Make a backup. In Settings, open Data & Backups and tap Export Marble Backup. Save the JSON file wherever you like.

Backups and moving to a new phone

The backup file includes exercises, sets, supplements, workout sessions, weigh-ins and your split. To restore, use Restore From Backup in the same screen. A restore adds missing data without deleting anything. If a restore fails, it leaves no partial changes behind.

Progress photos and videos stay on the device. They aren’t included in backup files.

The trade-offs

  • No automatic sync. marble doesn’t sync between devices. To move your history, export a backup and restore it on the other device.
  • Your copy is your responsibility. With no server, there’s no copy held by marble. Export a backup from time to time.
  • Optional connections are yours to choose. If you connect Apple Health, the data you send there is governed by Apple Health.
  • This website is different from the app. marble-fit.app uses anonymous, aggregated Vercel Analytics. The app does not.

If marble ever can’t open its saved data, it keeps the original files and offers a retry and a recovery copy instead of quietly opening an empty journal.

Questions

Does marble work without an internet connection?

Yes. Logging, parsing, analysis and storage all work offline on your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no marble account and no sign-up.

Does marble sync with iCloud or between devices?

No. marble doesn’t sync. To move your history, export a JSON backup and restore it on the other device.

Does marble show ads or sell my data?

No. The app has no ads and no third-party tracking, and your training data stays on your device.
